Welcome! This page is all about making drones more helpful and practical for your applications. It particularly focuses on making sense of drone data for agriculture and ecology. Take a look, and use whatever is helpful for you! 

If the methods are helpful for you, we would be extremely grateful for a cite! Methods can be found in our new preprint, shown below!

Parker, T. A., Celebioglu, B., Watson, M., & Gepts, P. (2025). Drone methods and educational resources for plant science and agriculture. bioRxiv, 2025-05. doi: https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.05.19.654604
